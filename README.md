# AI for Family Life: A Beginner's Manual

A plain-English, step-by-step guide to using free AI models for family life — school forms, meal ideas, activities, and bedtime stories — without ever pasting your kids' names or school information into an AI chat.

## What's inside

- `docs/` — the full beginner manual in Markdown (printable and easy to read)
- `site/` — the same manual as a static HTML website you can open in any browser
- `scripts/verify.sh` — checks the site and docs are complete and correct

## Who this is for

Parents, grandparents, and caregivers who want practical help with everyday family admin and who want to keep it private. No technical background needed.

## Privacy promise (read first)

See `docs/privacy-safety.md`. The short version: never paste your kids' names, your school's name, addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying family details into an AI chat. Use placeholders like `[Child's name]` instead.
