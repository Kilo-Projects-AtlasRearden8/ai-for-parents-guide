# AI for Family Life — Welcome

This manual shows you how to use free AI models for everyday family life: school forms, meal ideas, activities, and bedtime stories. Everything here is written in plain English, step by step, with no jargon and no assumptions about what you already know.

## The one rule to remember

**Never paste your kids' names, your school's name, addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying family details into an AI chat.** Use placeholders like `[Child's name]` and `[School name]` instead. Read more in [`docs/privacy-safety.md`](privacy-safety.md).

## How to use this manual

1. Pick one topic that would help you this week. School forms are a good place to start if you have paperwork piling up.
2. Read the section, then follow one step-by-step example.
3. Try it yourself with a real (but anonymized) task.
4. Print a planner from `docs/printable-planners.md` or `site/printable-planners.html` and keep it on the fridge.

## Free models you can use

You do not need to pay for AI to follow this manual. Good free options include:

- **Local models you run at home** (nothing leaves your computer): [Ollama](https://ollama.com) with models such as Llama 3 or Gemma
- **Free web chatbots**: free tiers of popular AI chat assistants (check the privacy settings and turn off "train on my data" where available)
- **Library or school-provided tools**: some libraries and schools offer free AI access to patrons and families

All examples in this manual work with any general-purpose AI chatbot. You do not need a specific brand.
