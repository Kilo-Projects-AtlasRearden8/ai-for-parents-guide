Privacy & Safety

This is the most important section of the manual. Read it before you use AI for anything family-related.

The golden rule: never paste your kids' names, your school's name, your home address, phone numbers, email addresses, birthdays, or any other identifying family details into an AI chat.

Why

AI chats are processed by computers you do not control. Even with privacy settings turned on, you should treat everything you type as if it could be seen by strangers. Protecting your family's privacy matters more than any convenience an AI offers.

How to do it right — use placeholders

Before you paste anything into an AI chat, replace real details with brackets:

Instead ofType
My child, age 6"[Child's name], age [age]"
My child's teacher at our elementary school"[Teacher's name] at [School name]"
Our home address"[Address]"
Our family email address"[Email address]"
"September 14 pickup at 3:15 pm""[Date] at [time]"

When the AI gives you an answer, fill in the real details yourself on paper or in your own documents — never inside the chat.

School forms

School forms often ask for names, addresses, and emergency contacts. Do not paste the whole form into an AI chat. Instead:

  1. Copy only the blank questions or instructions, with names removed.
  2. Ask the AI to explain what the form is asking or to draft an answer.
  3. Fill in the real form yourself with your family's actual details.

Medical information

Education only, never medical advice. This manual and the Asdale Dynamics, LLC guides provide education only. Do not paste medical records, diagnoses, or medication details into an AI chat. If you have health questions about your child, ask your pediatrician or nurse.

Bedtime stories

It is tempting to ask AI for a story "about my child who loves trains." Do not do that. Ask for "a story about a child who loves trains" and add your child's name yourself when you read it aloud.

Checklist before you hit send

  • [ ] No school names or addresses
  • [ ] No phone numbers, emails, or birthdays
  • [ ] No medical details
  • [ ] I understand the AI can make mistakes, and I will double-check anything important
