AI for Family Life — A Beginner's Manual

A plain-English, step-by-step guide to using free AI models for family life — school forms, meal ideas, activities, and bedtime stories — without ever pasting your kids' names or school information into an AI chat.

What's inside

  • Getting started — pick a free AI model, start your first chat, write a good prompt
  • Privacy & safety — keep your family's information private while using AI
  • School forms — read forms, draft answers, track deadlines
  • Meal ideas — plan dinners from what's already in your pantry
  • Activities — rainy-day games, weekend outings, low-energy afternoons
  • Bedtime stories — make up stories with AI, personalize them safely
  • Printable planners — ready-to-print pages for meals, forms, activities, and stories

The one rule to remember: never paste your kids' names, your school's name, addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying family details into an AI chat. Use placeholders like [Child's name] and [School name] instead. Read more in Privacy & safety.

How to use this manual

  1. Pick one topic that would help you this week. School forms are a good place to start if you have paperwork piling up.
  2. Read the section, then follow one step-by-step example.
  3. Try it yourself with a real (but anonymized) task.
  4. Print a planner from Printable planners and keep it on the fridge.

Free models you can use

You do not need to pay for AI to follow this manual. Good free options include:

  • Local models you run at home (nothing leaves your computer): Ollama with models such as Llama 3 or Gemma
  • Free web chatbots: free tiers of popular AI chat assistants (check the privacy settings and turn off "train on my data" where available)
  • Library or school-provided tools: some libraries and schools offer free AI access to patrons and families

All examples on this site work with any general-purpose AI chatbot. You do not need a specific brand.
