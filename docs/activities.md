# Family Activities with AI

Stuck for things to do? This section helps you find activities for rainy days, weekends, and low-energy afternoons — including options that work well for ADHD-friendly, low-energy parenting.

## Step 1: Pick the kind of day you're having

- Rainy day stuck inside
- Sunny afternoon at home
- Weekend outing
- Low-energy day (everyone is tired, including you)

## Step 2: Ask the AI for ideas

Example prompts:

- "Give me 5 rainy-day activities for a 6-year-old and a 2-year-old that need no special supplies."
- "Give me 3 free weekend activities for a family in `[City/region]` — replace with your own area when you write it down."
- "Give me 3 low-energy activities for a tired parent and a 5-year-old, things we can do sitting down."

## Step 3: Choose and do

Pick one activity, gather what you need, and do it. You do not have to do all of them.

## Activity ideas by type

**No supplies needed:**
- "I Spy" around the house
- Storytelling circle (each person adds one sentence)
- Dance party to one song
- Pillow fort

**Low energy / sitting down:**
- Coloring or drawing together
- Looking at old family photos
- Reading picture books
- Watching clouds out the window

**Weekend outings (find these yourself, not from AI):**
- Library story hour
- Local park or playground
- Community center events

## ADHD-friendly tips

- Offer two choices, not twenty: "Do you want to draw or build a fort?"
- Set a timer: "Let's play for 15 minutes, then clean up together."
- Keep one activity bin ready with crayons, paper, and blocks.
- It is okay to do nothing. Rest is a valid activity.

## Printable planner

Print the "Weekly Activity Planner" from `docs/printable-planners.md` or `site/printable-planners.html` and write one activity per day.

> **Privacy reminder:** never paste your kids' names, your school's name, or any identifying family details into an AI chat. Use placeholders like `[Child's name]` instead.
