# Bedtime Stories with AI

Bedtime stories help kids wind down and feel safe. This section shows you how to use free AI to invent stories quickly, personalize them safely, and read them aloud.

Remember: **never include your child's real name or identifying details in an AI chat.** Ask for a story "about a child," then add your child's name yourself when you read it aloud.

## Step 1: Choose a story idea

Pick something your child likes: animals, trains, space, bedtime, overcoming fear of the dark, trying something new.

## Step 2: Ask the AI for a short story

Example prompt:

> "Write a 5-minute bedtime story for a young child about a brave raccoon who can't sleep. Keep it gentle and calming, with a happy ending where the raccoon falls asleep. No scary parts."

Other prompts to try:

- "A short bedtime story about a train that learns to go slowly."
- "A 5-minute story about a child who is nervous about the first day of school, with a reassuring ending."
- "A story about the moon saying goodnight to animals, one page at a time."

## Step 3: Read it aloud yourself

Copy the story to paper or read it from your phone. Add your child's name where it fits ("Once upon a time, there was a raccoon named [your child's name]..."). Use a slow, quiet voice. Pause for pictures if you want to draw them together.

## Step 4: Make it a routine

Keep a "Bedtime Story Log" (printable at the end of this manual) so you remember which stories your child loved and want to read again.

## Tips for reading aloud

- Read slowly. Bedtime stories are not a race.
- Let your child turn the pages or choose the next story sometimes.
- If the AI story has a word your child does not know, explain it simply or swap in a word they know.
- You do not have to use the AI story word-for-word. Skip parts, change names, or make up your own ending.

## What AI is not good at here

AI cannot know your child the way you do. If a story seems too long, too exciting, or just not right, put it away and try another. Trust your judgment over the computer.

## Printable planner

Print the "Bedtime Story Log" from `docs/printable-planners.md` or `site/printable-planners.html`.

> **Privacy reminder:** never paste your kids' names, your school's name, or any identifying family details into an AI chat. Use placeholders like `[Child's name]` instead.
