Getting Started with Free AI

This page walks you through your first AI conversation, step by step. No experience needed.

Step 1: Pick a free way to use AI

Choose one:

  • Run it on your own computer (private, nothing leaves your house): install Ollama, then download a free model such as Llama 3 or Gemma. This is the most private option.
  • Use a free web chatbot: sign up for a free tier of a mainstream AI assistant. In the settings, turn off options like "train on my data" or "improve the model with my chats" if they exist.

Step 2: Start a new chat

Open the app or website and click "New chat." You will see an empty text box where you can type.

Step 3: Write your first prompt

A "prompt" is just what you type to the AI. Keep it simple and say what you want.

Example prompt:
"I am a parent. Please give me three easy weeknight dinner ideas that use chicken and take under 30 minutes. Keep the instructions short."

Step 4: Read the answer and ask for changes

If the answer is not quite right, tell the AI and try again:

Example:
"That's helpful. Can you make one of them vegetarian, and write a shopping list for all three?"

Step 5: Save what you like

Copy the answer into a notes app, print it, or write it on one of the printable planners at the end of this manual.

Tips for better results

  • Be specific. "Dinner ideas" works, but "dinner ideas for a picky 5-year-old, under $10, ready in 20 minutes" works better.
  • One thing at a time. Ask for meal ideas, then ask for the shopping list in a second message.
  • Say how you want it formatted. "As a bulleted list," "as a table," or "in under 50 words."
  • You can say no. If the AI suggests something unsafe, expensive, or weird, ignore it and ask again.

Example first prompts for family life

  • "Help me write a short email to my child's teacher asking about a missing permission slip."
  • "Give me five rainy-day activities for a 7-year-old and a 3-year-old that need no special supplies."
  • "Write a 5-minute bedtime story about a brave raccoon who cannot sleep."
  • "What are three questions I should ask at parent-teacher conference?"

What to do if you get stuck

  • Re-read the answer slowly. AI answers sometimes sound confident but include mistakes. Always double-check anything that matters (dates, addresses, medication instructions, school deadlines).
  • Start a fresh chat if the conversation gets confusing.
  • This manual is written so any free AI model can handle the examples. If one answer seems off, try rewording your prompt more simply.
